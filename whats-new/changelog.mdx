Have an idea for what we should build next? [Suggest a Feature](https://app.enfue.com/job/enfuehelpdesk/featuresuggestion?view=public).

<Update label="Nov 1, 2026" description="Custom fields for jobs, Notes comments, and a Messenger upgrade">
  - **Custom fields for jobs:** add your own fields to jobs and fill them in when you create or edit a job. Export your jobs with their custom fields as a CSV from [Reports](/hiring/reports#share-and-export).
  - **Comments in Notes:** comment on a passage of a note, even while you're only reading it. Comments stay attached to the right text as the note changes. See [Notes](/hiring/notes#comment-on-a-note).
  - **Dock a note:** keep a note open beside your work, for example to take notes while you review applicants. See [Notes](/hiring/notes#keep-a-note-open-while-you-work).
  - **Emoji and attachments in Messenger:** add emoji and send files in your chats with candidates. See [Messenger](/hiring/messenger#send-a-message).
  - **Add existing applicants to a talent pool** straight from the pool. See [Talent Pools](/hiring/talent-pools#add-applicants-to-a-pool).
  - **Referral program:** the new referral program is now in Enfue.
  - Applicants you add are linked automatically to their Enfue account when one exists with the same email address, so you can chat with them in [Messenger](/hiring/messenger).
  - Your company profile hides the **Jobs**, **Internship**, and **Posts** tabs when they're empty. See [Career page](/workplace/career-page).
  - On the interviewer dashboard, **View Profile** now only appears for applicants with an evaluation waiting. See [Interviewers](/settings/users/interviewers#what-interviewers-see).
  - The applicant list stays fast with thousands of applicants, and applicant details open more quickly.
  - Many small improvements to the applicant details, the job board, evaluations, Notes, and the text editor.
  - Fixed searching your connections.
</Update>

<Update label="Oct 25, 2026" description="Email from your own account, a new Settings page, and company locations">
  - **Email from your own Google or Microsoft account:** send bulk emails to applicants from your own mailbox, with attachments, and bring in the last 90 days of email with your applicants in one click. Enfue now asks for your consent before it connects your account.
  - **A clearer Inbox:** better filters, a cleaner layout, and faster loading, with emails loaded page by page. See [Inbox](/hiring/inbox).
  - **Settings is now a full page with tabs,** instead of a side panel. See [Settings](/settings/overview).
  - **Name your offices:** give each company location a name, such as "Head office". Addresses you paste show on a Google map. See [Contact](/settings/general/contact#locations).
  - **More photos in About:** a new photos section on the **About** tab of your company page. See [Career page](/workplace/career-page#about).
  - **Plans:** subscriptions are billed yearly, **Lite** is now called **Starter**, and the payment page shows how much each payment method will charge. See [Subscription](/payments/subscription).
  - When you import an applicant, choose the job and talent pool to add them to. See [Adding applicants](/hiring/applicants/add-applicants).
  - The interviewer dashboard has a **View Profile** button and works better on phones. See [Interviewers](/settings/users/interviewers#what-interviewers-see).
  - Shared links to public jobs now open the right job page.
  - Fixed Inbox sections that kept expanding on their own.
</Update>

<Update label="Oct 18, 2026" description="Interviewer dashboard, custom field filters, and your own mailbox">
  - **Interviewer dashboard:** interviewers now land on their own dashboard instead of Home, with the interviews and evaluations that need them. See [Interviewers](/settings/users/interviewers).

  - The applicant full details now show the applicant's **LinkedIn** profile.

  - Fixed the navigation menu scrolling sideways.
